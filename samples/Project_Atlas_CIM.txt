Executive Summary
Northwind Analytics, Inc. (the "Company") is a leading provider of cloud-based supply chain planning software for mid-market manufacturers and distributors. Founded in 2009 and headquartered in Austin, Texas, the Company serves over 1,250 customers across 14 countries.
The Company is founder-owned and has grown revenue at a 24% CAGR over the last four years. Approximately 88% of revenue is recurring subscription revenue, and net revenue retention was 114% in FY2025.
In FY2025 the Company generated revenue of $62.4 million and Adjusted EBITDA of $17.8 million. EBITDA margin of 28.5% reflects a highly scalable model.
The top ten customers account for 11% of revenue. The Company employs approximately 340 full-time employees across 4 offices in the United States, United Kingdom and Germany.
Jane Whitfield, Founder and Chief Executive Officer, will remain with the business post-transaction.
Visit www.northwind-analytics.com for more information.
