# Deal Tracker: people leave, the deal history shouldn't

Private equity runs on memory that lives in a few senior heads, inboxes and personal spreadsheets. Deal Tracker captures that memory as a by-product of the work a deal team already does, and hands it to whoever comes next.

- **30%** of senior professionals left their firm at some point, in a study of 138 PE managers. Turnover among the professionals responsible for a deal is associated with weaker performance on that deal, though causality may run both ways. [1]
- **~10 years** is a typical fund life. Industry press describes PE as especially exposed to turnover because it depends on institutional memory over that long life. [2]
- **0** records of why a firm passed, in one vendor-reported case where the deal sold to a competitor 18 months later. [3]

## What walks out the door with a senior person
| Question | Knowledge lost |
|---|---|
| "Why did we pass?" | The valuation gap, the red flag, what would have changed the answer |
| "Who actually decides?" | Which founder trusts us, which banker sends us their best processes. Sourcing still runs on these calls and meetings. [4] |
| "Which model went to IC?" | v7 on someone's desktop, with no record of what changed from v6 |
| "What did we promise?" | Follow-ups agreed in management meetings, kept in a notebook |
| "Are we still bound?" | NDA confidentiality and return-or-destroy duties that outlast the deal [5] |
| "Have we seen this before?" | The same company back under a new codename through a different advisor |

## Why it hasn't been solved
Deal CRMs ask people to type, and busy people stop typing. In a 2009 survey of 61 PE funds, 37% used no formal CRM and ran on Outlook and Excel [6]. Vendors today still list "the pipeline lives in a shared Excel file or personal notebooks" as a warning sign, and say associates and partners abandon tools that need constant manual entry [7]. A system has to give value back right away, or the data never goes in [8]. The knowledge that matters most at a departure, the reasoning and relationships, is exactly what never gets entered.

## The product: capture memory while doing the work
- **Drop in a CIM.** Revenue, EBITDA, margins, growth, customers, HQ, CEO, advisor and deadlines are detected with the source sentence and page.
- **Every model version kept.** IRR, MOIC, multiples and leverage are read from Excel and compared with the previous version.
- **Passing asks why.** The reason, what would change the answer and a revisit date are recorded and resurface automatically.
- **"You've seen this before."** New companies and CIMs are checked against every prior look.
- **Relationships are owned.** Every contact has an owner at the firm, a strength and a meeting history. Cold relationships are flagged.
- **Nothing slips.** NDA end dates, overdue next steps, deals with no lead and stale processes appear in one list.

## The handover
Every live deal gets a **knowledge score**: lead, description, CIM facts, model, meeting notes, mapped people, and a next step or pass reason.
1. **See the gaps.** The handover pack lists every deal the person is on and what's undocumented.
2. **Close them.** The departing person fills the gaps while they still can.
3. **Generate the pack.** Deals, open questions, lessons, who to call and relationships to transfer, on one printable page.
4. **Reassign in one click.** Deals and relationships move to the successor, and each deal's timeline records it.

## Who it's for and how it's sold (proposed)
- Lower and core mid-market PE with 5–50 investment professionals, firms raising their next fund (LPs weigh team stability [1]), independent sponsors and search funds.
- Local edition free for one deal team. Team edition with a shared workspace, permissions and email/calendar capture, priced per seat. Optional AI analysis billed at cost through the firm's own Anthropic key. Pricing to be validated with design partners.

## Built today, and the next 90 days
**Built:** pipeline, companies, meetings, contacts and a network diagram. CIM fact detection that runs offline, with optional Claude analysis. Versioned models. Knowledge scores, pass records, prior-look warnings, NDA tracking, handover packs and one-click reassignment. A printable IC one-pager.
**Next:** pilot with 3–5 firms and measure knowledge coverage before and after. Add email and calendar capture and a shared team workspace. Run a handover drill: time how long a successor needs to get up to speed on an inherited deal, with and without the pack.

## The ask
We're looking for design-partner firms to run Deal Tracker on live deals for one quarter. You get a knowledge-coverage baseline for every deal team member and a handover pack for anyone who leaves.
